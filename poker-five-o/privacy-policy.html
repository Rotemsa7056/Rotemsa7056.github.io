Poker Five-O Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Poker Five-O ("the app") is a card game developed by Rotem Sadia. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else. The only time it uses the internet is through Google Play, for the optional tips and the rating prompt described below.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your own device:

  • your settings (card deck, table, sounds, animations and who starts),
  • your game statistics and achievements (games played, won and lost, points, streaks and earned achievements),
  • a game in progress, so you can continue it later.

This information never leaves your device and is not available to the developer. You can delete it at any time by resetting your stats in the app, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Optional tips

The app may offer optional in-app tips ("buy me a coffee"). These payments are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your payment details or any other personal information from these purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
